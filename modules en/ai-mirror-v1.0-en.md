## PROTOCOL OBJECTIVE ("TRUTH SERUM EFFECT")
Force a Large Language Model to apply the 8-layer Case Filter **to its own previous response** (or to a pasted output from another AI). Expose every spot where the AI invented facts to sound helpful, hid missing data behind passive voice, cited anonymous "experts," or presented an undated forecast as a completed act.

## CALIBRATION ANCHOR
- **Honest AI Standard (`E`):** *"My weights contain no dated document for event X; record status — UNIDENTIFIED REMAINDER."*
- **AI Hallucination Standard (`Cells 1, 14, 17, 18`):** *"As is well known, leading experts note a significant increase in the metric..."* — simulating knowledge through linguistic fog.
